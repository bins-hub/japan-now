Mount Fuji and Kawaguchiko planning

A clear view of Mount Fuji depends on cloud cover and visibility. Check morning weather and live cameras, and prepare a lakeside alternative if the peak is hidden.

Transport checks

Bus and rail options from Shinjuku have different reservation rules and arrival points. Check your last return connection.

Is Mount Fuji always visible?

No. Clouds can obscure the mountain even on otherwise fine days.
