Plan Tokyo by neighborhood

Combine Shibuya and Harajuku for a western-city outing, and Asakusa with Ueno for an eastern-city outing. Mount Takao is better planned separately because of the extra journey.

Seasons and transport

During cherry blossom and foliage periods, allow for crowds and check the correct station exit and last-mile route.

Can I see all of Tokyo in one day?

Major districts are spread out; choose one or two areas per day.
