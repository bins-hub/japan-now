About cameras and travel information

Does JAPULSE cover every prefecture?

The directory navigates all 47 prefectures. Visitor webcams are currently listed in selected prefectures; other regions include links to official government road-camera resources.

Are all listed cameras live right now?

No. A camera being listed does not confirm it is currently broadcasting. Check the publisher's player and timestamps.

Are these security CCTV feeds?

No. JAPULSE refers to openly published visitor webcams and government camera information, not private security monitoring systems.

Broadcast availability changes. Some road cameras provide periodically refreshed still images rather than live video.
