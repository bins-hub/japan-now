Separate the skyline picture from an observatory visit

How to read the view

Compare cloud cover, building outlines and changing evening light. Do not identify the feed as a particular observation deck without checking the broadcaster's location description. Haze, rain, reflections and image quality can all change distant detail.

Plan your visit

  1. Plan an Abeno or Tennoji visit around Tennoji and Osaka-Abenobashi stations.
  2. Abeno Harukas is a station-linked complex; distinguish an observation-floor visit from shopping and dining.
  3. Look up transport separately if also visiting Dotonbori. Buildings visible in a skyline picture are not necessarily close together on foot.

Does a clear skyline mean I can enter an observation deck?

Visibility does not establish admission. Check the facility's own hours, ticket information and entry restrictions before planning a visit.
