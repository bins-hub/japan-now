Plan Kojima Shrine around the day's tide information

How to read the view

The approach between shore and shrine changes with the tide. Even if a path appears on screen, recording time and delay prevent it from establishing access when you arrive. Separate scenery viewing from a crossing plan.

Plan your visit

  1. Check official tide-related visitor information for your actual travel date, not a low-tide time copied from another day.
  2. Plan transport from your arrival port on Iki and your return sailing separately. This is not a walking extension of Hakata Station or Okinawa's Bise area.
  3. Follow local access notices and conditions at the site. Do not use a path seen on camera as permission to cross; account for the return too.

Can I cross if the camera shows a path?

The picture cannot establish that. Check the official visitor information and local restrictions. A landscape feed is not access permission or a safety assessment.
