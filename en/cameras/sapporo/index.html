Choose a section of Odori Park before setting out

How to read the view

Odori Park extends east to west, so one picture cannot cover the whole park. Identify the visible block and camera direction. Snow visible in one section does not establish pavement conditions elsewhere, particularly from a distant view.

Plan your visit

  1. Compare the block you want to visit with the TV Tower at the eastern end.
  2. Connect that section to a nearby station exit. A short stop and a walk through the entire park need different route plans.
  3. For an event, check its designated area and visitor information rather than inferring operation from structures visible on camera.

Can one park view tell me the weather across central Sapporo?

It only shows part of one location. Shade and pavement vary between blocks and a feed may be delayed; compare it with the forecast for your route.

Beyond the camera: Odori Park, Sapporo

The TV Tower end of Odori Park is only part of a long east–west green space.

WATCH · Read the view

A camera showing the eastern blocks cannot confirm snow, events or restrictions across the entire park.

RUN · Move safely

Running conditions vary with festivals, snow and icy surfaces. Check current access and consider walking instead.

EAT · Choose locally

Restaurants around Tanukikoji and Nijo Market are in different directions; choose your route before selecting a place to eat.

Three steps for your visit

  1. Start by locating Odori Station and the TV Tower end of the park.
  2. Decide how far west to explore or whether to head toward a shopping or food area.
  3. Check seasonal event notices, footwear needs and return transport.

What the camera cannot tell you: A snow-free image does not establish safe footing in other park blocks.
