About this camera

The camera options link to official YouTube videos by NTV NEWS (Terminal 2) and TBS NEWS DIG (Terminals 2 and 1). Streams may end or prohibit embedding. JAPULSE does not record or rebroadcast these videos.

Use the airport picture separately from flight information

How to read the view

Check the T1 or T2 label in the broadcaster selector. Different camera directions can show different light and aircraft movement at the same time. One aircraft's movement cannot establish the status of your own flight.

Plan your visit

  1. Confirm your airline, flight number and terminal through the airport or airline first.
  2. Use that terminal as the destination for your train, monorail or bus search. T1, T2 and T3 are not interchangeable drop-off points.
  3. Check official inter-terminal transport if transferring. Plan aircraft viewing separately from check-in and boarding.

Does a lack of aircraft movement mean the airport has stopped?

One view cannot establish airport-wide operations. Check camera direction and playback; use the airport flight search or your airline for the status of your flight.

Beyond the camera: Haneda Airport, Tokyo

Use the aircraft camera for plane watching while relying on official airport information for every travel decision.

WATCH · Read the view

Different T1 and T2 camera views may show different activity; an empty runway view does not mean flights are cancelled.

RUN · Move safely

Airport terminals are not running routes. Prioritize check-in, security and safe walking routes.

EAT · Choose locally

Choose airport food by terminal and by whether the shop is before or after security. Some locations are not available to all passengers.

Three steps for your visit

  1. Confirm your airline, flight number and terminal from the ticket or airport website.
  2. Check official inter-terminal connections if you need to transfer.
  3. Choose an accessible meal stop and verify the gate and boarding time again.

What the camera cannot tell you: Video is not a live flight-status service and does not confirm delays or cancellations.
