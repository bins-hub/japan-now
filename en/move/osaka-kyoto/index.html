Frequently asked questions

Are Kyoto Station and Kyoto-Kawaramachi the same station?

No. Kyoto Station is JR; Kawaramachi is Hankyu in another area. Choose for your accommodation location.

Does Hankyu go directly to Kyoto Station?

Hankyu main central-Kyoto arrival is Kyoto-Kawaramachi, different from JR Kyoto Station.
