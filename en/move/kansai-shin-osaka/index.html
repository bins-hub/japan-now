Boarding rules and sources

Paper: buy an overseas agency voucher or reserve with JR-WEST, then collect at a station. Bring the payment card, reservation number, four-digit ID and passport for online bookings.

WEST-QR: buy from an overseas agency, register and activate on an online smartphone, then scan at gates. No paper exchange; reserve in its dedicated car.

Ordinary reserved or non-reserved accommodation is covered; Green class is separate. Exiting at an intermediate station invalidates the remaining journey.

Exact date-specific first and last departures: unverified.
