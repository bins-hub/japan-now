Frequently asked questions

Are JR Nara and Kintetsu Nara the same station?

They are in different locations. For Nara Park, compare walking and bus access from each station.

Does Kintetsu limited express cost extra?

Yes. An express ticket is needed in addition to ordinary fare; check the exact amount through official booking or fare search.
