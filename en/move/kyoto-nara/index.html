Getting from Kyoto to Nara

JR and Kintetsu are both possible depending on your departure station and destination. Kintetsu-Nara and JR Nara are separate stations with different approaches to the park.

Are JR Nara and Kintetsu-Nara the same station?

No. They are in different locations, so compare the route to your actual destination.
