Narita Airport to Shinjuku: how to get there

Shinjuku: a direct train or a change at Nippori?

Allow about 90 minutes on N’EX to Shinjuku, using the Tokyo tourism board’s guide. Waiting, immigration and the hotel walk are additional. The fastest train to Ueno is not automatically the best choice for a Shinjuku hotel.

Tickets and terminal access

Buy both a base-fare ticket and a limited-express ticket. Tapping Suica alone does not pay the express supplement.

Use Narita Airport Station for T1 and Airport Terminal 2・3 Station for T2/T3. The walk from the T3 arrivals information counter to the station gates takes about 13 minutes.

A date-specific exception

Collect the round-trip ticket with your original non-Japanese passport; travel Narita → Tokyo area → Narita. Compared with two ¥3,330 singles, it saves ¥1,460.

From the evening of 6 November to the morning of 8 November 2026, construction makes some N’EX trains terminate at Tokyo instead of Shinjuku. For 7 November, plan a change at Nippori or Tokyo rather than relying on a direct Shinjuku service.
