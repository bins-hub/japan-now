From the arrival station to your hotel

For a hotel around Ueno, compare the walk from Keisei Ueno first. If continuing on JR, change at Nippori to avoid moving between the two Ueno stations.

All seats are reserved. An IC card still needs a separate liner ticket. Standard singles for ages 6–11 cost ¥1,290; the online discount is ¥1,150.
