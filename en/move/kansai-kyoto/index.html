Kansai Airport to Kyoto: arrival tips

JR Haruka connects Kansai Airport with Kyoto Station. Ticket products and seat conditions vary, so check the operator and leave time for immigration and the platform.

Does Haruka take me straight to my Kyoto hotel?

No. From Kyoto Station you may need a subway, bus, taxi or walk depending on your hotel's location.
