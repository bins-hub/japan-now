Kansai Airport to Kyoto tips

Haruka reaches Kyoto Station, but ticket products have different seat conditions. Allow separately for immigration and platform access.

Does Kyoto Station arrival mean I am at my hotel?

No. Depending on your hotel, continue by subway, bus, taxi or on foot.
