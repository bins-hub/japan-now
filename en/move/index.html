Four checks on arrival

Use this checklist to choose real transport. Links go to airports and operators rather than guessing fixed fares or last-train times.

① Arrival terminal and station

Terminals 1, 2 and 3 and rail stations may be in different places. Check the arrival terminal on your ticket first.

② Immigration and baggage time

Landing time is not train boarding time. Allow for immigration, baggage collection and walking to the station.

③ Last train and late arrivals

For evening arrivals, check the last service for that date officially. After it, compare airport buses, taxis and nearby accommodation.

④ Final connection to your hotel

The arrival station may require another subway or walking connection to the hotel. Use the hotel-address input to check the complete journey.

Japan airport transfer questions

Which is more convenient for Tokyo: Narita or Haneda?

It depends on accommodation and arrival time. Haneda is generally closer to central Tokyo; Narita has major rail routes to Tokyo, Shinjuku and Ueno. Compare total hotel travel and transfers.

Is the train from Kansai Airport to Namba the same as to Kyoto?

No. Namba uses Nankai routes; Kyoto uses services such as JR Haruka. Operators differ even at the same airport station, so check signs.

Should I book a train for a late-night arrival?

Allow for flight delays and immigration. Last-train times depend on date and operator; check official timetables before reviewing booking conditions.

Is a transport pass always cheaper?

No. It depends on days, routes and number of trips. Compare an ordinary ticket if only travelling one way from airport to hotel.

Before your airport transfer

Rail access differs by terminal. Set travel date, time and exact hotel address in Google Maps. Prioritize official rail information for hours, fares and seat reservations.
