Frequently asked questions

Must I change for Kamakura?

Some JR Yokosuka trains run directly from Tokyo toward Kamakura. Check destination and stops in the timetable.

Does the same train reach Enoshima?

Kamakura Station and Enoshima are different locations. Continue by Enoden or another route.
