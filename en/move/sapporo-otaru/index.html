Frequently asked questions

Do all New Chitose Airport trains go to Otaru?

No. Only some run through to Otaru; check the destination.

Is Otaru Canal immediately outside Otaru Station?

A walk is needed after arrival. Check map directions from the station to Otaru Canal.
