Kansai Airport to Namba: how to get there

Transport and fares

Transport: Nankai Rapi:t / Airport Express

Time: Rapi:t fastest 34 minutes / Airport Express about 45 minutes

Fare: Rapi:t requires an express ticket in addition to base fare; regular express ticket ¥700

Reviewed 2026-10-28 · varies by date, section and ticket type.

Airport stations and transfers

At Kansai Airport Station board Rapi:t or Airport Express for Namba. Rapi:t requires an express ticket.

Immigration, baggage and hotel access are extra. Check the specific train and last service officially.
