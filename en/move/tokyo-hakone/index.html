Shinjuku to Hakone-Yumoto: trip planning

The Odakyu Romancecar is a reserved-seat limited express; it differs from local Odakyu services in price, duration and transfer arrangements. Compare options on the operator's current timetable.

Three checks before boarding

Confirm the departure platform and seat reservation, check the timetable for your travel date, and plan onward transport from Hakone-Yumoto to your specific onsen or sightseeing stop.

Alternatives and cautions

Weather and disruptions can change services. Check limited-express surcharges separately from base fares, and review what any transport pass actually covers.

Can I ride the Romancecar with only a basic train ticket?

No. The Romancecar requires checking its separate limited-express ticket and reserved-seat conditions.
