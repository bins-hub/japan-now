Does Romancecar require a separate express ticket?

Yes. A reserved express ticket is needed in addition to the base fare.

Is Hakone-Yumoto the final stop for my onsen accommodation?

Depending on accommodation, another mountain railway, bus or walk may be required.

Shinjuku to Hakone-Yumoto travel tips

Romancecar has different fares, durations and transfers from ordinary Odakyu trains. Choose between speed and savings and compare official timetables.

Three checks before leaving

Check the Shinjuku platform and reserved-seat requirement, your travel-date timetable, and onward connections from Hakone-Yumoto to the bath or sight.

Alternatives and cautions

Weather or disruption may change services. Distinguish the Romancecar surcharge from ordinary fare, and check pass inclusions.

Can I use Romancecar with only an ordinary ticket?

No. Check express-ticket and reserved-seat requirements in addition to ordinary fare.
