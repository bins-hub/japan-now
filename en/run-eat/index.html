Plan with published course information

Official Takano route ~4.3 km (one-way section)

How to follow the route

Follow the official route from Kamo Ohashi via Kawaibashi, Mikagebashi and the Takano River bridges toward Matsugasaki. Returning along the river adds distance beyond the stated 4.3 km.

Facilities and access

High river levels after rain can affect passage. Public baths and running stations have different baggage policies.

Food after the run

Masugataya mackerel sushi and Demachi Futaba sweets are in the Demachiyanagi area; check travel time if you finish at Matsugasaki.
