Who is this area for?

Explore the Yubatake-centred town and traditional bathing culture. Yubatake and Sainokawara Park — combine these sights with a half-day walk or an overnight onsen stay. Public baths, private baths and accommodation differ by facility.

Getting there

Take a bus from Naganohara-Kusatsuguchi Station. Some properties offer a free shuttle; follow the selected property access instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take a day-use bath in Kusatsu?

The area has several types of baths, but not every property accepts non-staying visitors. Check the chosen facility day-use guide.

Can I visit with tattoos?

Japanese bath rules vary by facility. Check small covered tattoos and private-bath conditions separately with the selected bath.

Kusatsu visit checklist

Yubatake is the town landmark, but viewing/photo areas differ from bathing facilities. Check bath rules, towels and tattoos for each facility.

Can I enter the water at Yubatake?

No. Yubatake cools and distributes spring water and is a sightseeing attraction. Bathe only in authorized bathing facilities.
