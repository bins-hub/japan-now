Hakone onsen: before you visit

Day-use baths and overnight ryokan have different policies. Before booking, confirm day-use access, last admission, tattoo policy, private-bath reservations and towel availability directly with each facility.

Getting there

Odakyu trains connect Shinjuku with Hakone-Yumoto. Facilities in Gora or Sengokuhara may require an additional local train or bus transfer.

Important practical checks

Hours, closing days, prices, mixed/family use and children's rules depend on the operator. For a day trip, check your last return connection before departing.

Do all Hakone hot springs accept tattoos?

No. Tattoo policies vary by establishment. Contact the operator in advance or ask about a bookable private bath.

Questions before visiting

Can I visit without staying overnight?

Not every inn accepts outside visitors. Check the day-bathing information of the specific facility.

Can I enter with tattoos?

Rules vary. Small covered tattoos and private-bath use may have different conditions; ask the chosen facility.
