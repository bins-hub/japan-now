Who is this area for?

Combine mountain scenery, museums and an overnight hot-spring stay near Tokyo. Lake Ashi and Hakone Open-Air Museum — combine these sights with a half-day walk or an overnight onsen stay. Public baths, private baths and accommodation differ by facility.

From Odawara Station, continue by Hakone mountain railway or bus. Some properties offer a free shuttle; follow the selected property access instructions.

Day use · reservable private open-air baths

Hakone Yuryo (箱根湯寮)

Public bath adults: weekdays ¥1,700–1,800; weekends/holidays ¥2,000–2,200 / Elementary-school children 1,000 yen

Weekdays 10:00~20:00 (19:00 Admission closes)
Weekends/holidays 10:00–21:00 (admission closes 20:00)

About 3 minutes by free shuttle from Hakone-Yumoto Station, or 5 minutes on foot from Tonosawa Station.

Private open-air baths cost extra and can be reserved. Preschoolers cannot use the public bath; check private-bath admission instead.

Tenzan Onsen (天山湯治郷)

Adults 1,450 yen · Children 700 yen (Age 1 through elementary school)

09:00~23:00 (22:00 Admission closes) · No regular closing day; possible closure for about five days in mid-December

Take the K-route bus from Hakone-Yumoto to Okuyumoto-iriguchi, then walk about 5 minutes. Cash only.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take a day-use bath in Hakone?

The area has several types of baths, but not every property accepts non-staying visitors. Check the chosen facility day-use guide.

Can I visit with tattoos?

Japanese bath rules vary by facility. Check small covered tattoos and private-bath conditions separately with the selected bath.

Before using a Hakone bath

Ryokan stays and day-use baths have different conditions. Before booking, check day admission, last entry, tattoo rules, private-bath bookings and towels with each facility.

Getting there

Odakyu trains connect Shinjuku with Hakone-Yumoto. Baths in Gora, Sengokuhara or other districts may need a local bus or mountain-railway transfer.

What should I check?

Closing days, fees, male/female bathing schedules and child rules differ by facility. Check the last return transport before a day trip.

Do all Hakone baths accept tattoos?

No. Rules vary; advance inquiries or a private bath may be necessary.
