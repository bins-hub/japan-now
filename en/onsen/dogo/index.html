Who is this area for?

Combine the historic bathhouse buildings with a stroll through Matsuyama’s shopping arcade. Dogo Onsen Honkan and Haikara Street — combine these sights with a half-day walk or an overnight onsen stay. Public baths, private baths and accommodation differ by facility.

Getting there

Take a Matsuyama city tram to Dogo Onsen Station. Some properties offer a free shuttle; follow the selected property access instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take a day-use bath in Dogo?

The area has several types of baths, but not every property accepts non-staying visitors. Check the chosen facility day-use guide.

Can I visit with tattoos?

Japanese bath rules vary by facility. Check small covered tattoos and private-bath conditions separately with the selected bath.
