Shibu Onsen

A historic hot spring town in Yamanouchi, Nagano Prefecture.

Before you visit

Access to the nine public bathhouses may be restricted to overnight guests; day visitors should check eligible baths individually.

Choose an actual facility

  1. Choose the district and decide between a day visit and an overnight stay.
  2. Choose a facility on the map, then compare fees, opening hours and booking conditions on its official website.
  3. If a private bath or tattoo acceptance is essential, use the individual facility’s policy.

Questions before visiting

Can I visit without staying overnight?

Not every inn accepts outside visitors. Check the day-bathing information of the specific facility.

Can I enter with tattoos?

Rules vary. Small covered tattoos and private-bath use may have different conditions; ask the chosen facility.
