Shibu Oyu day bathing, admission and access

A mountain hot-spring town with traditional lanes and public baths. This guide covers local sights, access and conditions to check before bathing.

Who is this area for?

A mountain hot-spring town with traditional lanes and public baths. Shibu Onsen streets and the area near Jigokudani Monkey Park — combine these sights with a half-day walk or an overnight onsen stay. Public baths, private baths and accommodation differ by facility.

Getting there

From Yudanaka Station, continue by bus or taxi. Some properties offer a free shuttle; follow the selected property access instructions.

Facilities covered by official information

Day-use facility

Shibu Oyu (渋大湯)

Adults ¥800 · Children ¥500

Usual guide: 10:00–17:00 · hours may differ by weekday

This is the only Shibu public bath open to non-staying day visitors. The other eight are for overnight Shibu guests. Buy an admission ticket and arrange unlocking at the designated nearby facility. Shampoo and body wash are not provided.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take a day-use bath in Shibu?

The area has several types of baths, but not every property accepts non-staying visitors. Check the chosen facility day-use guide.

Can I visit with tattoos?

Japanese bath rules vary by facility. Check small covered tattoos and private-bath conditions separately with the selected bath.
