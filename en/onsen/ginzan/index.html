Bus from Oishida Station

Hanagasa Bus Oishida–Ginzan costs ¥1,000 adult / ¥500 child one way. No booking; pay cash, not IC.

Example on an ordinary day: Oishida 09:50 → Ginzan 10:26; bathe and eat, then Ginzan 13:25 → Oishida 14:01. Adult bus return plus Shirogane-yu is ¥2,500, excluding meals. Do not use this bath plan on Wednesday.

Bus timetable validity: 2026-10-25–2027-03-27.

Parking, shuttle and winter access

Day drivers park at Taisho Romankan and use the paid shuttle. General guidance: free parking, adult day shuttle ¥500; Romankan departures 09:00–17:00, last Ginzan departure 18:00.

Separate 2026–27 winter admission and booking rules: unverified. Restricted winter shuttles have been announced separately from the general service; past winter tours are not current bookable products.

Frequently asked questions

Can I reserve a day bath?

No. The tourism association states day bathing is not reservable. Shirogane-yu is the lower-priced option; on Wednesday compare another bath’s operating day.

Tattoos, private baths and child prices?

Facility-specific tattoo acceptance, private baths and child admission are unverified. The child bus fare is not the bath admission price.
