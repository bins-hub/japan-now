Frequently asked questions

Can I take a day-use bath in Beppu?

The area has several types of baths, but not every property accepts non-staying visitors. Check the chosen facility day-use guide.

Can I visit with tattoos?

Japanese bath rules vary by facility. Check small covered tattoos and private-bath conditions separately with the selected bath.

Choosing a Beppu day-use bath

At Takegawara, choose ordinary bathing or a sand bath separately. Hyotan public and family baths have different booking, price and operating conditions.

Are Beppu Hells sightseeing and bathing the same?

No. The sightseeing Hells and actual baths are different facilities. Do not enter the hot water at the Hells.
