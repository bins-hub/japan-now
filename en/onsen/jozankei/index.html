Who is this area for?

A valley retreat that combines nature with a hot-spring break on a Sapporo trip. Futami suspension bridge and valley walk — combine these sights with a half-day walk or an overnight onsen stay. Public baths, private baths and accommodation differ by facility.

Getting there

Take a Jozankei-bound bus from Sapporo. Some properties offer a free shuttle; follow the selected property access instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take a day-use bath in Jozankei?

The area has several types of baths, but not every property accepts non-staying visitors. Check the chosen facility day-use guide.

Can I visit with tattoos?

Japanese bath rules vary by facility. Check small covered tattoos and private-bath conditions separately with the selected bath.
