Who is this area for?

Hot-spring inns line the Hida River in this mountain town. Hida River and hot-spring streets — combine these sights with a half-day walk or an overnight onsen stay. Public baths, private baths and accommodation differ by facility.

Getting there

Use JR Gero Station. Some properties offer a free shuttle; follow the selected property access instructions.

Steps for choosing a facility

  1. First choose an area near Hida River and hot-spring streets and decide between an overnight stay and a day visit.

  3. If private bathing or tattoo acceptance is essential, use the individual facility policy, not general area information.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take a day-use bath in Gero?

The area has several types of baths, but not every property accepts non-staying visitors. Check the chosen facility day-use guide.

Can I visit with tattoos?

Japanese bath rules vary by facility. Check small covered tattoos and private-bath conditions separately with the selected bath.
