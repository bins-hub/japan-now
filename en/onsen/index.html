Are day visits available?

Some baths welcome non-staying visitors. Check the card price, then confirm closures and last admission for your visit date on the official website.

What will it actually cost?

Prices shown are standard adult rates or conditional minimums. Check whether bathing tax, towels, reserved family baths, night surcharges and parking cost extra.

Can a family bathe together?

A public bath and a reserved private bath are different services. Even where family baths are listed, check separate reservations, charges and time limits.

What about tattoos and swimsuits?

Tattoo rules vary; unverified facilities are not marked as accepting tattoos. Ordinary baths usually do not permit swimsuits, though some special facilities differ.

Onsen questions for first-time visitors

Can I enter without a reservation?

Some public day-use baths accept walk-ins, while hotel baths and private baths may require reservations or an overnight stay. Check the selected facility.

Should I bring towels and shampoo?

Towels and toiletries may be free, rented or unavailable. Bringing your own is useful at public baths; check the individual facility guide.

Can I enter with tattoos?

There is no universal tattoo policy. Size, covering stickers and private-bath use can affect admission; ask the facility or check its official website.

Can children use a family bath?

Some properties offer reservable family baths or in-room baths. Check child age restrictions, extra charges, group size and booking times.

Can I take photos inside?

Do not use phones or cameras in changing or bathing areas, to protect privacy. Take photos only in areas where the facility permits it.

General guidance is based on the JNTO official onsen guide.

How to choose an onsen

Use the actual bathing facilityto check admission, day-use access, private-bath reservations and tattoo rules, rather than relying on area-wide assumptions.

JNTO onsen guide ↗

Check bathing rules before visiting

  • Day-use access, last entry, closing days, tattoos and child admission differ by bath. Prioritize the official facility guide.
  • Wash before entering the bath, keep towels out of the water, and follow the facility rules.
  • For a day trip, check both last bath admission and the last return transport. Consult a clinician about health concerns such as illness or pregnancy.
