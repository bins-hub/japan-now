Does a cloudy Fuji camera mean no view all day?

One Kawaguchiko image cannot describe the whole day. Postpone a view-only transfer and compare forecasts and the original again at your next planning time.

Can city temperature decide summit clothing?

Kawaguchiko/Sapporo city forecasts are not Fuji or Moiwa summit observations. Use the destination guide’s altitude and operation information for a mountain visit.
