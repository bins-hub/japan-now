Where to find live cameras in Japan

To see a particular location such as Shibuya in Tokyo or Dotonbori in Osaka, open the regional camera list, then follow the provider’s original YouTube link. JAPAN NOW brings public camera information and source links from several areas together in one guide.

How to check whether a broadcast is live

A camera information page opening does not mean the camera is currently broadcasting live. On the original YouTube page, check the LIVE indicator, whether the picture is recent and whether the broadcast has ended. Some videos may be recordings or temporarily interrupted streams.

If the video will not play

Some broadcasts cannot be embedded on external sites or may have regional restrictions. If playback fails within this site, try the original YouTube link and source page shown on the camera guide.

Use cameras as supporting information

Live cameras are a supplementary reference for weather and local atmosphere. Check official local guidance for important decisions about road restrictions, overall crowd levels, or climbing and swimming safety.

Frequently asked questions

Are Japan’s live cameras free to view?

JAPAN NOW’s camera information pages are free to read. External video platforms have their own terms, and mobile data charges are separate.

Do the cameras broadcast 24 hours a day?

It depends on the broadcast. Continuous live coverage is not guaranteed; check each provider’s actual status.

More practical tips

Choose a city

Open the Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto or Mt. Fuji guide, then select the camera of interest. Each camera has its own page and source information.

Play within JAPAN NOW

Use the play control in the embedded player. Some streams may be unavailable due to the broadcaster’s schedule or restrictions. Try the next camera button if one is offered.

Verify the stream

Check the video publisher's LIVE badge or recording date. A player loading successfully does not prove that the picture reflects the current moment.

Use other information too

Webcam footage is limited to one angle. Weather warnings, train disruptions and venue opening hours should be checked through official providers.
