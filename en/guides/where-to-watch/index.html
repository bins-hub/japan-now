Where to watch Japan live cameras

How to find a public camera, confirm that the video is currently LIVE and watch Japanese city scenery on JAPAN NOW.

Choose a city

Open the Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto or Mt. Fuji guide, then select the camera of interest. Each camera has its own page and source information.

Play within JAPAN NOW

Use the play control in the embedded player. Some streams may be unavailable due to the broadcaster’s schedule or restrictions. Try the next camera button if one is offered.

Verify the stream

Check the video publisher's LIVE badge or recording date. A player loading successfully does not prove that the picture reflects the current moment.

Use other information too

Webcam footage is limited to one angle. Weather warnings, train disruptions and venue opening hours should be checked through official providers.
