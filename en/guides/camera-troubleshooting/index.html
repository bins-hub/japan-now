Why Japan live cameras may not play

Solutions for unavailable livestreams, restricted YouTube embeds, buffering and stopped cameras.

The player says the recording is unavailable

The livestream may have ended or the video ID may have changed. Reload the page or select another camera; a previously published link is not guaranteed to remain valid.

The player shows an embedding error

A publisher may prohibit third-party playback, or YouTube may reject missing origin/referrer data. JAPAN NOW uses the official YouTube embedded player and cannot override the publisher’s restrictions.

Playback freezes or stays black

Check your network connection and browser, disable interfering extensions, then try again. On mobile, tap Play directly if automatic playback is blocked.

Report a problem

Send the affected camera page URL and error message using the contact page. Avoid including sensitive personal data.
