Why Japan live cameras may not play

Solutions for unavailable livestreams, restricted YouTube embeds, buffering and stopped cameras.

The live broadcast may have ended

A YouTube livestream may end or its video ID may change. A camera information page appearing in the directory does not guarantee that the video is currently live.

It plays on YouTube but not on this site

If the broadcaster does not allow external embedding, playback may be blocked in this site’s player. Open the camera page’s original YouTube link to try the source directly.

A black screen on mobile

Network conditions, browser permissions, extensions or platform restrictions may affect playback. Try another browser and check the original source link.

Check whether the picture is current

Do not assume a video is live from an old thumbnail or title alone. Check the original service’s LIVE indicator, publication time and broadcast status.

Frequently asked questions

Does every listed camera always play?

No. Broadcast interruptions, regional restrictions or external playback restrictions may apply.

What should I open first if there is an error?

Check that camera’s original YouTube link and source page first.

More practical tips

The player says the recording is unavailable

The livestream may have ended or the video ID may have changed. Reload the page or select another camera; a previously published link is not guaranteed to remain valid.

The player shows an embedding error

A publisher may prohibit third-party playback, or YouTube may reject missing origin/referrer data. JAPAN NOW uses the official YouTube embedded player and cannot override the publisher’s restrictions.

Playback freezes or stays black

Check your network connection and browser, disable interfering extensions, then try again. On mobile, tap Play directly if automatic playback is blocked.

Report a problem

Send the affected camera page URL and error message using the contact page. Avoid including sensitive personal data.
