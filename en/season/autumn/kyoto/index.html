Kyoto autumn foliage: practical planning

Autumn colors in Kyoto vary by year, elevation and site. Choose Arashiyama for riverside scenery, Tofuku-ji for temple viewpoints, or Kiyomizu-dera for hillside walks.

Check before departure

Review current weather and recent foliage updates. Allow additional travel and queue time during peak weeks, and verify temple hours and special openings on official sites.

Suggested half-day route

From Kyoto Station take JR to Saga-Arashiyama, explore the riverside, then return toward central Kyoto for either Tofuku-ji or Kiyomizu-dera. Visiting all three usually works better as a full-day plan.

Is Kyoto's 2026 autumn color peak date confirmed?

No. Peak color depends on temperature, elevation and tree species; forecasts are not verified on-site observations.
