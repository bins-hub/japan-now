Access and conditions by place

Maruyama Park

Walk five minutes from exit 3 of Maruyama Koen on the Tozai subway. The park stroll is separate from the mountain hiking trail. The park centre opens 09:00–17:00; large-bag coin lockers cost ¥500 per use.

Nakajima Park

Use Nakajima Koen on the Namboku subway. There is no visitor car park; use public transport.

Choose a half-day plan

Park-focused two hours (editorial suggestion): walk Maruyama for 60–90 minutes, then eat or visit a café nearby. Walking admission ¥0; transport and food extra.

Viewpoint half day (editorial suggestion): walk Nakajima, then take public transport to the ropeway base for a Moiwa return trip. Adult facility cost ¥2,100; transport, food and waiting extra.

Moiwa hours and access

April–November ropeway: 10:30–22:00, last ascent 21:30. Mini cable car: 10:30–21:50, last ascent 21:40. The return combo includes both.

The free shuttle runs every 15 minutes from 17:17 on weekdays or 10:17 on weekends/holidays (10:47 on winter weekends). At weekday lunchtime, plan the roughly 0.5 km / 10-minute walk from its stop to the base.

The sightseeing road closes for winter from 2026-11-21. That is distinct from ropeway suspension. Search for the Sapporo Moiwa Ropeway base station, rather than the mountain summit.

Travel questions

Should I walk all three in half a day?

Choose one park, or one park plus Moiwa. Woodland, pond and elevated views offer different experiences; let your interests and transfer time decide.

Is the peak date confirmed?

Weathernews, labelled as supplied by Maruyama Park, reports peak colour at 70–80% on 2026-10-28. Leaf fall around 4 November is a forecast; the 30 October on-site state is unverified.
