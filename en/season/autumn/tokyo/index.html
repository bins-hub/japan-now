Seasonal travel questions

Is the best viewing date guaranteed?

No. Bloom, foliage and snow conditions vary with temperature, altitude and location. A forecast is different from an on-site observation. Check current conditions before leaving.

What if I have only half a day?

Choose one area around the first destination, leave time for food and rest, and check your return transport.

Tokyo foliage: pick the right area

Koishikawa Korakuen is a garden walk, Meiji Jingu Gaien is known for its ginkgo avenue, while Mount Takao is a mountain hike. Plan Mount Takao as a separate outing rather than rushing all three.

Where should I go for photos?

Consider Meiji Jingu Gaien for ginkgo trees and Koishikawa Korakuen for garden scenery. Check access restrictions and crowd information on your travel date.
