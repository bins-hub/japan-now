Kyoto winter travel planning

Snowfall in central Kyoto is variable and a snow-covered temple view is never guaranteed. Check the weather forecast and live local conditions before travelling.

Winter walking tips

Temple approaches can include slopes and steps. Use grippy shoes and allow more time in cold or wet conditions.

Choosing an area

Consider one district such as Arashiyama or Higashiyama at a time instead of rushing across the city.

Is snow guaranteed in Kyoto in winter?

No. Snow cover depends on the weather and can disappear quickly.
