Tokyo Winter scenery · seasonal travel guide

Tokyo winter weather

December 7.7°C · January 5.4°C · February 6.1°C. These are Japan Meteorological Agency 1991–2020 monthly climate normals for Tokyo, not the forecast for your travel date. Bring a coat and scarf; it feels colder after sunset.

Ueno Park

Reference photo, not a live view · Nesnad, CC BY 4.0 / Wikimedia Commons

Metropolitan park area: 05:00–23:00, free to walk around. Approximately 2 minutes on foot from JR / Tokyo Metro Ueno Station, or 1 minute from Keisei Ueno Station. Nearby museums have separate opening hours and admission fees.

Shinjuku Gyoen

Reference photo, not a live view · Mycomp, CC BY-SA 4.0 / Wikimedia Commons

1 October–14 March: 09:00–16:30; last entry 16:00. Normally closed Mondays (the next weekday if Monday is a holiday), and 29 December–3 January. Adult admission ¥500; children aged 15 and under are free. Student and senior discounts have eligibility requirements.

Marunouchi and Tokyo Station

Reference photo, not a live view · Procrastinator acc, CC0 1.0 / Wikimedia Commons

Street walking is free. Combine the garden during daylight with Tokyo Station / Marunouchi after sunset. Individual 2026–2027 illumination events have separate confirmed dates; a suggested evening walk does not confirm an event is running.

Rail transport

JR Yamanote Line is one option between Ueno and Tokyo stations. Compare the actual route from Shinjuku Gyoen to Ueno based on your departure point and the travel date.

Suggested one-day itinerary

10:00–11:30 Shinjuku Gyoen → 11:30–13:00 lunch around Shinjuku → 13:00–15:30 Ueno Park → after 16:00 Tokyo Station / Marunouchi for food and night views. Add transport time between places. This is a suggested schedule, not train times or measured journey durations. Museum admission and opening times are separate.

Admission budget

Ueno Park walking: free. Shinjuku Gyoen: adult ¥500. Marunouchi street walking: free. Minimum ordinary admission for these three walks is therefore ¥500 per adult; transport, meals, museums and paid events are additional.

Can I plan on seeing snow?

Central Tokyo does not have persistent winter snow. Parks, night views and indoor museums are more dependable activities than planning a trip solely for snow.

What is the simplest route?

Morning Shinjuku Gyoen → afternoon Ueno Park → evening Tokyo Station. Check the garden’s 16:00 winter last entry and the separate opening times of any museums you add.
