Choose an indoor or viewpoint plan

Indoor-focused half day (editorial suggestion): Shiroi Koibito paid visit 10:30–12:30 → lunch → short Odori walk. Adult facility cost ¥1,200; choose this to limit outdoor walking.

Viewpoint half day (editorial suggestion): one city park → Moiwa return trip. Adult facility cost ¥2,100. Give one evening attraction enough time rather than fitting both winter lights and Moiwa into the same evening.

Family facility example: two adults plus one elementary-school child pay ¥3,000 at Shiroi Koibito’s paid area or ¥5,250 for Moiwa return travel. Transport, meals and extra workshops are excluded.

Shiroi Koibito admission and winter lights

The free garden and shopping zone need no admission ticket or reservation. Paid entry is ¥1,200 for high school and above, ¥600 from age four through junior high, and free at three and under. Workshops can require separate products and bookings.

The announced 2026–27 Sweet Illumination is planned for 2026-12-01 to around late March 2027, with free viewing 17:00–19:00. The exact end date is unverified. Evening garden access does not extend paid-exhibit admission.

Moiwa winter operation and access

December–March ropeway 11:00–22:00 (last ascent 21:30); mini cable car 11:10–21:50 (last ascent 21:40). Closed 31 December and 1 January; annual maintenance is scheduled for 2027-03-29–04-28.

Shuttles run every 15 minutes from 17:17 on weekdays and 10:47 on winter weekends/holidays. Earlier, plan the roughly 0.5 km walk from the shuttle stop to the base, adding time for snow. The sightseeing road closes for winter from 2026-11-21.

Travel questions

Can I arrive at 17:00 and visit the factory?

Paid admission ends at 16:30. For a 17:00 arrival, choose the free illumination during its event period or an open free area, not a paid factory visit.

Is snow scenery certain in winter?

Same-day snow and visibility observations are unverified. Use weather and camera pages for the outdoor decision; if views are obscured, choose the indoor exhibit plan.
