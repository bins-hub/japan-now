About JAPAN NOW

Learn about JAPAN NOW, an independent directory of Japanese public webcams and destination guides.

What is JAPAN NOW?

JAPAN NOW is an independent guide to publicly linked cameras around Japan, combined with practical destination information and walking ideas.

Who owns the video?

Video streams and thumbnails belong to their publishers. JAPAN NOW does not claim ownership or relay camera streams through its own server.

How is the guide produced?

Destination descriptions are written to help visitors research attractions, transport and local food. Operations, prices and broadcasts can change and should be confirmed with original sources.

Editorial approach

We identify known landmarks, link relevant source pages and avoid presenting unverified camera footage as guaranteed live content.
